Ball knowledge: which mechanic do we build next?

Right now the only way the game rewards actually knowing football is chemistry — spotting when two of your drafted players share a club. I want more ways to reward the art of knowing your stuff, and I trust you two more than any focus group. Read the four options, then cast your votes at the bottom.

To: James & Ramsay From: Ben Re: 8and0.app Reply by: whenever, over a pint

1 where we're at

What's already on the pitch

Quick lay of the land so your vote is informed. Three things already exist — the new ideas build on this foundation rather than start from scratch.

Live now

Chemistry. Two players from the same real club quietly lift your team's rating. It's deliberately hard to farm from random nation spins — a flex, not a crutch.

Live now

Higher or Lower. A side quiz — guess which of two players is rated higher. Tracks your best streak. Proof people enjoy a pure knowledge test.

In the data, unused

Fame ≠ quality. Every player carries a "fame" score separate from their rating. The gap between who casuals recognise and who's actually good is sitting there waiting to be used.

2 the shortlist

Four ways to reward ball knowledge

Each one rewards a different kind of knowing. Tap “Make this #1” on your favourite as you read — that feeds the ballot at the bottom.

A

Scout's Eye

Reward spotting the best player — when the game won't tell you

In blind/hard mode ratings are hidden. If you pick the genuinely best player available for that slot without being shown the numbers, you get a “Perfect Pick” — a small boost and a streak. Pure eye for a player.

EVIDENCE — Blind mode already hides ratings, so the hard part is built. This just scores whether your gut was right. Rewards the exact skill: knowing who's good.

Effort: Low · uses what's already there
B

Knowledge is currency

Answer trivia mid-draft to earn an edge

When a nation lands, get a quick question about that country (“Portugal's all-time top scorer?”). Nail it and you win an extra re-spin — or, in blind mode, you get to reveal one hidden rating. Knowledge you can literally spend.

EVIDENCE — Higher or Lower proves the quiz is fun on its own. This wires it into the main game so knowing your stuff changes the draft, not just a side score.

Effort: Medium · needs a trivia bank
C

Chemistry 2.0

Reward the deep cuts, not just club-mates

Go beyond “same club.” Reward a real national spine (club + country together) and famous real-life partnerships — a keeper and his centre-back, a classic full-back/winger duo. The stuff only a proper fan spots.

EVIDENCE — Chemistry already works and players like it. This widens what counts as knowledge while keeping it hard to fluke from random spins.

Effort: Medium · needs a partnerships list
D

Ball Knowledge Rating

One number that grows the more you show you know

A personal stat that climbs across games — from perfect picks, spotting underrated gems, chemistry built, quiz streaks. A second leaderboard that isn't about your team's rating, it's about you. Turns a vibe into a score you chase.

EVIDENCE — The abstract idea becomes tangible and competitive. Best built last, on top of A–C, since it counts up everything they produce.

Effort: Higher · sits on top of the others
